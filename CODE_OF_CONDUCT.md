# Code of conduct

We want this project to be welcoming and productive. Be respectful, assume good
faith, and keep discussion focused on the work. Harassment, personal attacks and
discrimination are not tolerated.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that break these
rules and may ban repeat offenders. To report a problem, contact the
maintainers privately through the repository's Security tab or the email on
their GitHub profile.

This is adapted from the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
